10×10 · Ten kinds of warrior
Goal: checkmate. Trap the enemy King so it can’t escape. Kings are never captured.
Friend games
Rulebook: all 10 warriors

Win by checkmate: attack the enemy King so it cannot escape, block, or capture its attacker. A King is never captured. Stalemate (no legal move while not in check) is a LOSS for the trapped side. After 60 plies with no capture or Spearman move, the side with more material (a Spearman or more) wins. Draws: same position 3 times, or only Kings left. No castling, no en passant. Green numbers = squares moved. Red ring = capture. Orange dashed = siege shot (the Trebuchet stays put). Tap any piece, even an enemy’s, to preview it (blue).
